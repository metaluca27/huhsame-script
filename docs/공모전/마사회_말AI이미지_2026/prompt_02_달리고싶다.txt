Acrylic painting on canvas, in the same painting style as the reference images: flat stylized layered clouds with soft edges, bold solid black silhouettes, vivid sunset gradient of orange, magenta, violet and gold, visible acrylic brush texture. Scene: Jeju Island at sunset, eulalia grass and a basalt stone wall in black silhouette at the bottom. In the lower part, a young woman in a light jacket and jeans sits on a walking horse, both black silhouettes, while a guide walks ahead holding the lead rope; she sits up straight, gazing toward the horizon. In the sky above, the layered clouds themselves form the shape of the same woman and horse galloping at full speed into the wind, painted in glowing gold and cream cloud forms with the mane and her hair streaming, as her imagination of running free. The contrast between the slow walk below and the free gallop in the clouds is the heart of the image. Vertical composition. No text, no signature, no watermark.
