# 01. Slow Morning (초안)

모델: Popcorn 3.0 Pro (245cr)

## 스타일
Laid-back jazz hip hop with lo-fi warmth, relaxed and cheerful slow morning mood. Swung boom bap drums around 85 BPM with soft brushed snare. Warm, airy female vocal, smooth and easygoing, half-sung and half-spoken. Rhodes electric piano chords, round upright bass, muted jazz guitar licks, a little vinyl crackle. Gentle intro, the groove settles in, catchy singable chorus, soft outro that fades out. Cozy, sunny, unhurried texture.
